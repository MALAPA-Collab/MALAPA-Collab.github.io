# Subscribe

Get announcements about upcoming MaLAPA workshops from the **malapa-announce** mailing list.

| | Click for a pre-filled email | Or email `listserv@fnal.gov` with this body |
|---|---|---|
| **Subscribe** | [:material-email-plus-outline: Subscribe](mailto:LISTSERV@fnal.gov?body=SUBSCRIBE%20malapa-announce) | `SUBSCRIBE malapa-announce Your Name` |
| **Unsubscribe** | [:material-email-remove-outline: Unsubscribe](mailto:LISTSERV@fnal.gov?body=SIGNOFF%20malapa-announce) | `SIGNOFF malapa-announce` |

!!! warning "Reply **OK** to the confirmation email to finish subscribing. Unsubscribe from the same address you subscribed with."

You can also create an optional account at [listserv.fnal.gov](https://listserv.fnal.gov) to subscribe, unsubscribe, or change your settings online.
